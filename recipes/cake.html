Healthy Banana Cocoa Oat Cake (Sugar-Free&Flourless)

A delicious healthy cake that you can make at any time for any occasion! Not only is it healthy, but it's also incredibly tasty.

Ingredients:

Dry Ingredients:

  • 120g rolled oats
  • 18g Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp Baking Soda
  • 1 Pinch Salt

Wet Ingredients:

  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 70-80ml milk
  • 1/4 cup coconut oil or olive oil, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. In a large bowl, mix the dry ingredients.
  3. In another bowl, combine the wet ingredients.
  4. Gradually add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients, stirring until just combined.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  7. Let cool before serving.
